What counts as a contract

200 active or newly signed paid employment or staff-augmentation contracts facilitated through TAQAT and BrightGaza, each backed by a verifiable contract or formal engagement record. One-off gigs stay out of the headline number.

After month 6: 30-50 new contracts a month from repeat hiring, multi-hire accounts and referrals.
